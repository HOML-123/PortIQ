PROJECT NAME: PortIQ

CATEGORY: Work & Automation

PROJECT TYPE: AI-Powered Logistics Intelligence and Decision Support Platform

DESCRIPTION:

PortIQ is a logistics operations decision-support platform that combines shipment data intelligence, rule-based disruption risk analysis, what-if simulation, machine learning anomaly detection and BharatCode-powered AI explanations.

The platform helps logistics teams inspect shipment records, identify high-priority exceptions, understand potential operational consequences and compare possible recovery actions.

BHARATCODE AI INTEGRATION:

PortIQ integrates the BharatCode Qwen model through the BharatCode OpenAI-compatible API. The AI Operations Analyst receives structured operational evidence from the platform and generates explanations and decision-support analysis.

TECHNOLOGY:

Python, Streamlit, Pandas, NumPy, Plotly, Scikit-learn, SQLite and BharatCode Qwen.

CORE FEATURES:

1. Shipment Intelligence
2. Disruption and Risk Intelligence
3. AI Operations Analyst
4. What-if Disruption Simulator
5. Recovery and Action Planner
6. Decision Audit and Reporting
7. Machine Learning Anomaly Detection

DATA TRANSPARENCY:

The built-in demonstration dataset and disruption scenarios are simulated. User-uploaded CSV and Excel files are supported. The prototype does not claim live carrier, port or vessel-tracking integrations.
